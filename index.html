Architectural Dining Experience

Designed for those who savor intimacy, elegance, and unhurried moments.

Born from a desire to redefine fine dining, Chill merges minimalist dark aesthetic with world-class culinary innovation. Our restaurant was built around the principle that a dinner should be a sensory ritual—from the weight of hand-forged cutlery to the subtle warmth of acoustic soul soundscapes.

Under the guidance of Executive Chef Antoine Vance, every ingredient is selected from small-batch organic farms, sustainable fisheries, and rare vintage cellars.

Wood-Fired Precision

Smoked with oak, hickory, and Japanese Binchotan charcoal.

Craft Alchemy
